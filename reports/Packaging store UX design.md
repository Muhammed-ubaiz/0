# Win Packaging Buyers With Visible Unit Economics

Ideal Pack should run one bilingual catalogue that shows every buyer the carton price, the piece count, the per-piece cost and the fit-critical size on every product card and page, with a public quantity-price ladder and pack-or-carton choices. **No UAE store examined visibly showed carton and per-piece prices together, while WebstaurantStore does**, so this is the clearest opening in the market. The overflowing category bar and separate All Categories button should merge into one visible row with a labelled "More", and search should treat "500ml", "500 ml" and "500 cc" as the same thing. Compliance comes before any of this: public prices must include VAT, website information and invoices must be available in Arabic, and the catalogue needs an audit against single-use plastic bans already in force. If revenue is below AED 50m, B2B e-invoicing adds a hard deadline of 31 March 2027 to appoint a provider. Nearly every recommendation depends on structured data for each product, which is why cleaning 377 records is the highest-leverage work after compliance. The main limit is evidence: no study measures conversion for packaging stores, so the design advice below combines general usability research, competitor practice and judgement, and the largest bets should be tested on Ideal Pack's own traffic.

## UAE rivals leave the per-piece maths to the buyer

Packaging stores fall into four models. Hotpack and Falcon Pack run parallel retail and wholesale catalogues, WebstaurantStore and Uline run one catalogue with pack sizes and quantity tiers, Superior Pack and Cater Pack route buyers to an enquiry, and Hotpack Bahrain and Multipack put a WhatsApp route for bulk orders beside retail prices. The table compares what could be confirmed.

| Store | Navigation pattern | Pack and unit price | Retail and wholesale | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Hotpack UAE](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/) | Promo strip, phone, hours, EN/AR, 17-entry category menu plus a separate main nav | Pack count in title; [carton data in a spec tab](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/products/aluminium-container-3-compartment-225x177x30mm-500-pieces); unit-price field blank in capture | Top-level "Wholesale" and "Shop Retail"; [538-result wholesale collection](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/collections/wholesale) | Page text read |
| [Falcon Pack](https://falconpackonline.com/product-category/wholesale/page/15/) | ["Shop by usage"](https://falconpackonline.com/en/category/shop-by-usage/restaurant) path; header unseen | Structure in titles, such as "1 Carton X 1000 Pieces" | Separate "WHOLESALE-" and "RETAIL-" product records | Search extracts only |
| [Multipack](https://multipackinternational.com/product-category/aluminium-products/containers/) | Promo strip, phone, cart with running AED total | "Packing" field in four formats on one page | Wholesale price as free text, with WhatsApp or call | Page text read |
| [Superior Pack](https://superiorpackuae.com/products/category/kraft-box-and-containers) | Enquiry cart, WhatsApp, category sidebar by product form | "MOQ: 5 ctn", no prices | Enquiry-led, with a separate shop link | Page text read |

| [Uline](https://www.uline.com/BL_407/Multi-Depth-Boxes) | [Industry hub](https://www.uline.com/industry/FoodandBeverage) plus catalogue tree; header unseen | Model grids with inside dimensions, per-box price and tiers | One catalogue; [volume above top tier goes to phone or quote](https://www.uline.com/CustomerService/ULINE_FAQ_Ans?FAQ_id=305) | Search extracts, some old |

Three patterns stand out. The UAE norm is a contact-forward header, with phone, email or WhatsApp visible on every regional site that could be read. Navigation recurs along two axes, material or form on one and audience on the other, as in [Hotpack Bahrain's cafe, office and clinic tiles](https://hotpack.bh/). And the economics of buying more stay hidden locally: Hotpack sells a [20-box pack at AED 35 and a 100-box pack at AED 155](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/products/multipurpose-cardboard-corrugated-e-commerce-shipping-boxes), which works out to AED 1.75 versus AED 1.55 per box, but the extract shows no statement of that saving. WebstaurantStore's [kraft container at $34.99 per 200 or $12.99 per 50](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/choice-8-1-2-x-6-x-2-kraft-microwavable-folded-paper-2-take-out-container-case/795PTOKFT2.html) puts the same choice in front of the buyer. **No UAE site read showed a formal public tier table.**

Treat this comparison with care. Pages were captured as text, so sticky behaviour, column counts, visual hierarchy and mobile layouts were not observed on any site, and "not seen" does not mean absent. Hotpack's unit-price line may render on a live page. Most Baymard figures cited below were seen only in search excerpts of its pages, and they measure how common a problem is, not what it costs in revenue.

## One category row should replace the overflowing bar

The current header offers two routes to the same content and hides part of one. NN/g describes horizontal scrolling on desktop as [one of the few interactions that consistently draw negative responses](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/horizontal-scrolling/), and an older NN/g study found desktop users opened hidden menus in [only 27% of cases](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/hamburger-menus/), using visible or combined navigation almost twice as often. No controlled study compares a mega-menu with a category bar or tests "More" overflow directly, so the pattern below is inferred, but the inference is one-directional: keep categories visible and never scroll them sideways.

| Zone | Recommended contents | Change from today |
|---|---|---|
| Thin utility strip | A delivery promise Ideal Pack can honour, WhatsApp and phone with Gulf-time hours, "العربية / English" text toggle, Track Order, For Business | New |
| Main row | Logo, search, WhatsApp, Account, Lists, Cart with total | "Chat" named for its channel; wishlist doubles as saved lists for business buyers |
| Category row | Main categories that fit, each opening a simple grouped panel, then "More" and Shop by Business | Replaces All Categories and the overflowing strip |
| Sticky state on scroll | One opaque row: logo, search, cart | Collapses the stacked header |
| Mobile | Menu, logo, search, cart; categories as top-level items in an expandable menu | WhatsApp must not float over Add to Cart or the filter button |

Items should move into "More" by measured width rather than clip at the edge. Dropdowns should open on hover with a **300-500 ms delay**, which [62% of sites lack](https://baymard.com/research-articles/ecommerce-navigation-best-practice). With 377 products, one level of grouped panels (by type, size range or lid option) is enough. The sticky row follows NN/g's advice to stay [short, opaque and distinct from the content behind it](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/sticky-headers/), and Baymard warns that [sticky chat on mobile often blocks critical content](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-ux-best-practices).

Keep "Aluminium", "Black Base", "Clear Containers", "Kraft & Eco" and "Paper Tableware" as the primary axis, since buyers recognise these terms and Hotpack Bahrain uses [a "Black Base Containers" tile](https://hotpack.bh/) too. Add a small second axis for cafes, restaurants, catering and bakeries. Avoid Hotpack UAE's [17-entry menu](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/), which mixes materials with "New Arrivals" and "Miscellaneous Products". On the homepage, a price-point tile such as "Under AED 10" is a cheap idea from [Hotpack's "UNDER 9 AED STORE"](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/), and banner text should be live text so it works in both languages.

WhatsApp earns its header place. In a YouGov survey of 1,000 UAE residents commissioned by Zbooni, [65% had used WhatsApp for a product or service inquiry in the past year, against 55% for call centres and 48% for email, and 87% wanted a real person rather than a bot](https://communicateonline.me/news/85-percent-of-uae-residents-want-businesses-to-use-whatsapp/). The survey dates from 2024, comes from a messaging vendor and measures stated preference, not purchases. No research was found on where chat or wishlist should sit, or on showing a cart total versus an item count, so the existing cart total can stay. It matches [Multipack's running AED total](https://multipackinternational.com/product-category/aluminium-products/containers/) and must remain VAT-inclusive for public shoppers.

## Size-aware search matters more than a bigger search engine

The documented failure closest to this catalogue is unit handling: **[54% of benchmarked sites have trouble with abbreviation and symbol searches](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-search-query-types)**, and shoppers can conclude an item is not stocked when these fail. Baymard calls abbreviations [the easiest type to support, because they mainly need term mapping](https://baymard.com/guidelines/344-search-queries-that-include-abbreviations-and-symbols). Ideal Pack should build that map before paying for a search engine: ml, cc and milliliter; l, ltr and litre; oz and ounce; g, gm and gram; mm and cm; and 8x10, 8 x 10, 8*10 and 8 by 10 as one form. The local need is concrete, because Falcon lists [430 cc](https://falconpackonline.com/product/wholesale-falcon-alum-container-rectangle-with-lid-8357-155-1-carton-x-1000-pieces/), Hotpack uses [mm and ml](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/collections/aluminium-containers), and Falcon also names [some items in oz](https://falconpackonline.com/product/souffle-container-clear-with-lid-1-oz-20-packs-x-100-pieces/). Trade synonyms (box and carton, tub and container, kraft and brown paper) and Arabic product terms should come from Ideal Pack's own search logs. Arabic query handling could not be tested on any competitor, so this area has no benchmark.

Autocomplete should show real products with thumbnail, size and pack quantity plus category links, which is cheap at this catalogue size. It should tolerate misspellings, which [69% of sites handle poorly](https://baymard.com/blog/offer-autocomplete-suggestions-for-misspellings), and copy the active suggestion into the field, which [58% do not](https://baymard.com/blog/copy-search-suggestion-to-search-field). Suggestions should [lead only to queries with good results](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/site-search-suggestions/). Queries such as "TRN invoice", "delivery", "minimum order" and "custom printing" should reach help content, since [66% of sites struggle with non-product searches](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-search-query-types). A no-results page should be [unmistakable and restate the query in an editable box](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/search-no-results-serp/), then offer the closest sizes labelled as near matches. Pre-filling the bar with trending terms raised [purchase incidence 10.4% in a field experiment](https://www.informs.org/News-Room/INFORMS-Releases/News-Releases/New-Study-Finds-Prepopulated-Search-Bars-Can-Significantly-Boost-Online-Sales), but on a giant Asian platform, so it is a test idea and not a default.

Filters should be few and specific to each category. Hotpack's [Quantity, Size and Option (Base, Base + Lid, Lid) facets](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/collections/aluminium-containers) match how a kitchen chooses, but its Size facet mixes mm, ml and "Deep (...)" in one list and its rating-count facet adds little on a 37-product page. Ideal Pack should separate dimensions from capacity, and offer pack quantity, lid option, material and price, adding microwave or freezer suitability where these differ. Any attribute shown on a card should also be filterable, a test [38% of sites fail](https://baymard.com/research-articles/have-filters-for-list-item-info). A filter should disappear when all products share one value, and a [peer-reviewed lab study](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mksc.2025.0261) is a reminder that more filters are not automatically better. Sort options should add **price per piece and size or capacity** alongside relevance and pack price. On mobile, filters belong in [their own layer, opened by a button that stays visible, with a sticky apply control](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-filter-ui) that shows the live result count.

## Every card and product page should answer pack, piece and fit

Pack-based pricing makes unit price the central listing fact, and **[86% of sites omit it](https://baymard.com/blog/price-per-unit)**. A card should read "AED X / carton of 500 · AED 0.xx each · 225×177×30 mm · Base + Lid". WebstaurantStore supplies the [case-and-each pairing](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/775/clear-hinged-take-out-containers.html) and Hotpack the [base-and-lid option](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/products/aluminium-container-3-compartment-225x177x30mm-500-pieces). Hotpack's unqualified ["From" prices](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/collections/wholesale) are the thing to avoid, since "From 25.00" says nothing when packs range from 10 to 1,000 pieces. Baymard's B2B study supports [product tables for buyers comparing precise specifications](https://baymard.com/blog/use-product-tables-for-desktop-product-listings), so aluminium and clear ranges suit a [Uline-style grid](https://www.uline.com/BL_407/Multi-Depth-Boxes) of size, capacity, carton quantity, price and add-to-cart. That study covers electronics on desktop, so the table should launch as a toggle and be tested against the grid. Baymard advises [against quick views on spec-driven products](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-quick-views), which argues for dropping them.

All of this requires one structured record per product: pieces per pack, packs per carton, length, width, height, capacity, material, thickness, lid option, and Arabic fields. Multipack's [four "Packing" formats on one page](https://multipackinternational.com/product-category/aluminium-products/containers/) show the cost of free text, while Falcon's ["20 Packs x 100 Pieces"](https://falconpackonline.com/product/souffle-container-clear-with-lid-1-oz-20-packs-x-100-pieces/) shows the right model trapped in a title. Even WebstaurantStore lists [66 oz in specs and 71 fl. oz in the title](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/choice-8-1-2-x-6-x-2-1-2-kraft-microwavable-folded-paper-3-take-out-container-case/795PTOKFT3.html) on one product. Generating titles, cards and filters from the same record prevents that.

The product page should stack the decision in order: a title with dimensions and count, the pack or carton selector, the price ladder, stock and dispatch date, then Add to Cart beside a pre-filled WhatsApp link carrying SKU and quantity. Specifications belong near the top in a table, [with units on every value and plain labels](https://baymard.com/blog/product-spec-sheet-ux-design). Dimensions must say inside or outside, as [Uline separates them](https://www.uline.com/Product/Detail/S-4201/Corrugated-Boxes-200-Test/20-x-20-x-20-Corrugated-Boxes), and lids need their own ratings, because [Uline's foil container base is rated to 450°F but its lid only to 180°F](https://www.uline.com/Product/Detail/S-25386/Take-Out-Containers/Aluminum-Foil-Take-Out-Containers-7-Round). Hotpack's ["Ask An Expert" and "Specifications" tab](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/products/aluminium-container-3-compartment-225x177x30mm-500-pieces) are good regional baselines. Strikethrough prices and "only 5 left" messages should appear only when true, because UAE penalties reach [AED 100,000 for a misleading price announcement](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/have-a-website-application-or-other-digital-platform-in-the-uae-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-uae-consumer-protection-law/). Mobile needs one sticky priority per page, usually Add to Cart. Pages should meet [Core Web Vitals thresholds](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) (largest content within 2.5 seconds, interaction response within 200 ms, layout shift at or below 0.1), with Arabic fonts subsetted.

## One catalogue with a public ladder serves both buyers

Ideal Pack should keep one product page per item with Pack and Carton as purchase options, not parallel catalogues. Hotpack's [538-result wholesale collection](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/collections/wholesale) and Falcon's duplicate records hide the saving, and Falcon [showed different prices for the same fork carton](https://falconpackonline.com/product/falcon-plastic-fork-black-pp-1-carton-x-1000-pieces/) on category and product pages. Whether blended stores outperform separate ones is unproven, since no comparative study turned up. At 377 products, though, duplication has little to recommend it.

No controlled evidence settles public versus gated wholesale pricing, and no source addresses whether a visible bulk price cannibalises retail orders. The case for openness is still stronger here. NN/g's older guidance is to ["show the price; and if the price is variable, offer common pricing scenarios"](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-trust-from-b2c/), [WebstaurantStore's tiers appear on public listing pages](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/search/catering-petites.html), and commodity packaging is easy to price-check anyway. The fair argument for gating, [scraping and supplier price agreements](https://wizcommerce.com/blog/b2b-ecommerce-website/), applies mainly to negotiated prices and third-party brands under such agreements, which Ideal Pack should check per supplier. The recommended structure has three levels: public pack prices and standard ladders for everyone; light registration for business invoicing, saved details and lists; and approved accounts for negotiated prices and credit. A public-ladder trial on a subset of products would generate the evidence nobody has published.

The ladder should be a small table of quantity, price per carton, price per piece and saving, highlighting the current row and nudging toward the next tier. Baymard observed that [limits surfaced only at the cart caused failures, and that missing pricing or stock information led participants to abandon a product](https://baymard.com/blog/b2b-electronic-components-machinery-launch), so the minimum and pack multiple should be visible up front and the stepper should move only in valid multiples. Decide deliberately whether tiers apply per item or across a family. On Shopify, [rules apply per variant, so a cafe could not pool 8 oz and 12 oz cups](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/catalogs/quantity-pricing) unless sizes are handled differently.

Repeat buying needs its own fast path. At this catalogue size, and with restaurant owners rarely knowing SKUs, "Buy Again" with last quantity pre-filled, current price and stock should come first, followed by a name-led bulk-add table on [Adobe's name-or-SKU model](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-admin/b2b/introduction). CSV upload can wait. Lists should be shareable, because in Baymard's testing [one participant copied a cart by hand and then gave up, and others found no way to send a list to colleagues](https://baymard.com/blog/b2b-electronic-components-machinery-launch). Evidence that reorder tools lift repeat rate is absent, and the often-repeated 30-40% figure [has no traceable source](https://btng-studio.pages.dev/articles/the-ultimate-guide-to-ux-design-for-b2b), so this rests on the logic of consumable purchasing. Quotes should be the exception: above the top tier or for custom printing, created from a cart or list, with a promised response time and a [downloadable written quote like Uline's](https://www.uline.ca/CustomerService/ULINE_FAQ_Ans?FAQ_ID=305). Retail shoppers should see only a small "Buying in bulk?" prompt.

Business identity should be captured when it pays off: an optional "Buying for a business?" checkout step that takes the company name and Tax Registration Number (TRN) and issues the correct invoice, copying the spirit of [Hotpack's "TRN Invoice Request"](https://www.hotpackwebstore.com/). The Wholesale page must state real minimums, tier thresholds, delivery by carton and payment terms, which is where UAE sites read were weakest; [Cater Pack's FAQ, for one, promises wholesale but lists no tiers or minimums](https://caterpack.co/). Credit is normal but risky: Atradius reports [credit sales at about 47% of UAE B2B transactions and about two in five invoices paid late](https://atradius.de/newsroom/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-in-united-arab-emirates-2026). Terms should come after approval and order history, with a short application and a stated decision time. Company accounts can start with one company, several delivery locations and two roles.

## UAE rules set the floor, and some deadlines have already passed

Public prices must [include VAT, and supplies to VAT-registered businesses may be priced exclusive only if that is clearly stated](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-arab-emirates/tax/vat-inclusive-displayed-prices-rule-exceptions-and-implications). An excl.-VAT view therefore belongs behind a verified business account, with every price labelled. Website information, product data, contracts, advertising and invoices [must be in Arabic, with other languages optional](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/uae-consumer-protection-regime-revamped), and a missing Arabic invoice, contract or advertising is reported to carry [AED 100,000](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/have-a-website-application-or-other-digital-platform-in-the-uae-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-uae-consumer-protection-law/). The header described has no language toggle, so this is likely the largest gap. "No returns" clauses are [prohibited as harmful terms for B2B and B2C alike](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/have-a-website-application-or-other-digital-platform-in-the-uae-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-uae-consumer-protection-law/). Sellers must [issue a detailed invoice, deliver on declared timeframes and logistics costs, run a complaints process and declare any digital-payment fee in advance](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/digital-commerce-platform-compliance-the-new-penalty-framework-for-uae-platforms/). The e-commerce law [reaches social media](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102iqny/uae-federal-law-on-modern-trading-by-technological-means/), so a sale closed on WhatsApp still needs the invoice and terms. Official texts could not be opened, so these points come from law-firm summaries and need review by UAE counsel or a tax agent.

The plastics ban is a catalogue issue that UX cannot design around. From 1 January 2024 single-use plastic shopping bags were banned, [including those labelled biodegradable, and from 1 January 2026 the ban covers plastic beverage cups and lids, cutlery, plates, straws, stirrers and Styrofoam food containers; single-use bags of any material under 50 microns are also banned](https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-to-ban-more-single-use-plastic-items-from-january-1-1.500381533). The reported list does not name PET or black-base plastic containers, but the text of Decision 380 could not be read, so confirm those ranges. The status of [PLA and plastic-lined paper remains unconfirmed by any government source](https://ecofy.io/resources/compliance/uae-single-use-plastic-ban/), which directly affects the Kraft & Eco and Paper Tableware ranges. Filters and badges for "biodegradable" or "food-safe" should exist only where a supplier document is on file for that product, given the reported [AED 250,000 penalty for incorrect descriptions](https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/have-a-website-application-or-other-digital-platform-in-the-uae-here-s-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-uae-consumer-protection-law/) and [MoIAT's food-contact certificate and migration-test expectations](https://moiat.gov.ae/en/media-center/news/2023/08/21/rep-workshop).

E-invoicing covers B2B sales but [leaves B2C out of scope for now](https://www.hawksford.com/insights-and-guides/uae-e-invoicing). A business under AED 50m must [appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 31 March 2027 and go live on 1 July 2027, with late appointment or implementation at AED 5,000 per month](https://www.hawksford.com/insights-and-guides/uae-e-invoicing). A business at or above AED 50m has already passed its 30 October 2026 appointment date. Ideal Pack's revenue was not supplied, so confirm the phase. Checkout needs two invoice paths and a validated 15-digit TRN field.

Payments should lead with Apple Pay and cards. Published cash-on-delivery shares [range from about 23%](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/23/3224680/28124/en/UAE-Quick-Commerce-Market-Report-2026-2031-Emirati-Millennials-Drive-Quick-Commerce-Growth-with-On-Demand-Lifestyle.html) [to 32% of orders](https://www.axiomxgroup.com/blog/cash-on-delivery-uae), so the only safe conclusion is that it remains material while declining. Keep it, but with [order-value limits and WhatsApp verification](https://www.axiomxgroup.com/blog/cash-on-delivery-uae), both vendor suggestions. Tabby or Tamara suit consumer baskets and should be hidden for business accounts unless the provider confirms otherwise. Business buyers need bank transfer against a pro-forma invoice. Show "AED" as text next to or instead of the [new dirham symbol, encoded only in September 2026](https://blog.unicode.org/2026/02/from-central-bank-to-code-point-roadmap.html), until device fonts catch up.

Arabic must be real right-to-left design, not a translated overlay. Prices, phone numbers and dimensions stay left-to-right inside Arabic text, isolated [with `<bdi>`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/HTML/Element/bdi), and [navigation arrows are mirrored while logos and numerals are not](https://specification.website/spec/i18n/rtl-support/). Test checkout, steppers, price ladders and "225 × 177 × 30 mm" specs first. Separate URLs for each language keep Arabic pages indexable. No credible data sizes the Arabic-speaking share of UAE shoppers or the conversion lift, and a [Dubai study of ATM users](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2024.1355632/full) suggests non-Arab residents overwhelmingly favour English. English can stay the default experience, but Arabic is a legal requirement regardless of demand.

## Sequence the work by legal exposure, then data, then interface

Rank within each stage reflects legal risk, relevance and likely cost, not measured lift, because no study supplies effect sizes for a catalogue like this.

| Stage | Action | Area | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now | Audit products and eco or food-safe claims against plastic bans | UAE | Press quoting regulator; official text unread |
| Now | Confirm VAT-inclusive public prices; remove no-returns and unilateral-change terms | UAE | Law-firm summaries |
| Now | Arabic for product data, site information, terms and invoices; add language toggle | UAE, header | Law-firm summaries |
| By 31 Mar 2027 (if under AED 50m) | Appoint e-invoicing provider; capture TRN; split B2B and B2C invoice paths | UAE | Advisory firm summary |
| Next | Build the structured pack and spec record for all 377 products | All | Competitor failures; design judgement |
| Next | Carton, piece count and per-piece price on cards, product pages and cart | Listing, detail | Baymard prevalence; WebstaurantStore practice |
| Next | Unit and synonym mapping, product autocomplete, no-results recovery | Search | Baymard prevalence; NN/g |
| Next | Category-specific filters and price-per-piece and size sorts; mobile filter layer | Filters | Baymard; Hotpack practice |
| Then | One category row with "More", hover delay, compact sticky header | Header | NN/g and Baymard; no direct test |
| Then | WhatsApp with hours, pre-filled context and human handoff | Header, detail | Vendor-commissioned survey |
| Then | Pack or carton selector, public ladder, multiple-only stepper, dispatch date | Detail, wholesale | Baymard B2B (electronics); retailer practice |
| Later | Buy Again, shareable lists, business checkout step, quote from cart | Wholesale | Baymard observations; platform norms |
| Later | Table view, Shop by Business, delivery cut-off if operations can honour one | Listing, navigation | Competitor practice |
| Last | Approved credit accounts, two-role company accounts, CSV upload | Wholesale | Vendor surveys; low certainty for small buyers |

Instrument before launch so each stage can be judged: zero-result queries, search exits, filter use, carton-versus-pack mix, and repeat-order rate by account type. The first experiments should be the table-versus-grid view on aluminium and the public ladder on a product subset. A manual browser review of Uline, WebstaurantStore and Falcon Pack, and interviews with a few kitchen buyers, would close the biggest research gaps cheaply, including whether samples drive first orders, a hypothesis this research could not source.

## Conclusion

The storefront decision turns out to be mostly a data decision. Header polish is visible but the evidence for its revenue effect is thin, whereas per-piece pricing, unit-aware search, usable filters, Arabic statements, compliant eco claims and invoices all draw on the same product record. A 377-product catalogue is small enough to perfect that record by hand, an advantage over rivals running hundreds of separate wholesale listings.

The strategic opening is transparency. UAE competitors treat wholesale as a separate catalogue, a phone call or a line of free text, so a public, per-piece, VAT-clear price ladder would make Ideal Pack easier to trust and to compare, while WhatsApp keeps the human contact this market expects. The risk is unmeasured: nobody has shown what open bulk pricing does to margins or retail basket size, so Ideal Pack should treat that one choice as an experiment rather than a settled best practice.
